How it works

Every frame is earned.

PlayWin Snooker is where players challenge each other head-to-head, play it out, and let the scoreboard decide. No shortcuts — just the table and your cue.

Direct challenges — pick your opponent Live matches — play in real time Player ratings — reputation built on results

How a match works

Three steps between opening the app and racking up a win.

1

Send a challenge

Find an opponent and send a challenge. They accept, and the table's ready.

2

Play the frame

Settle it live, break for break, until someone clears the table.

3

Rate the match

Once it's over, rate your opponent. Ratings build a player's standing over time.

Built for competition, not chance

PlayWin Snooker is a skill-based platform. Outcomes are decided by how well you play — not by odds, not by luck.

Every match is player versus player, start to finish.
